# Design system

## Identity

**Passage Watch** — *Choose what your tokenized holding becomes.*

The design is a calm financial workbench: an exact decision, its evidence, and its unknowns. It is not a trading terminal, marketing site or chatbot.

IBM Plex Sans for prose and interface; IBM Plex Mono for quantities, identifiers and timestamps. Font files are OFL-licensed and will be self-hosted.

## Main composition

The first screen asks three things: the exact holding, the desired destination, and the deadline. Routes appear below as comparable records, not unrelated dashboard cards.

A destination switch is the primary moment. A plan-change view is the second moment. Evidence disclosure is quiet and precise.

## States

Use words, not only colour:

- Quoted
- Requires preparation
- Terms only
- Different destination
- Unknown applicability
- Conflicting evidence
- No quote from this provider
- Stale

## Accessibility

- Keyboard-complete primary flow.
- Visible focus and non-colour status labels.
- Mobile route records instead of a squeezed table.
- Reduced-motion support.
- No indefinite spinners or fake live counters.

## Diagram policy

Only architecture, outcome-route and plan-change diagrams ship. Charts and decorative network maps do not.
